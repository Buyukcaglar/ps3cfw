KNOWN CRITICAL ISSUES
- Short/failed reads must never become zero-filled successful metadata.
- Incomplete ownership/traversal must never authorize reclaim or clean markers.
- Current NetBSD EA handling and older disabled EA ownership need adaptation.
- cg_initediblk is an exclusive inode-slot prefix; corrupt-prefix arbitration is
  still open. Our migration core uses /512 di_blocks and does not automatically
  support the published 4096-unit accounting case.
- The real disklabel has full BE64 signatures and 0x30/0x90 entries; discovery
  heuristics in native and C# donors cannot authorize bounded repair.
- Fat NOR outer CBC and nested XTS need named profiles and explicit origins.
- FreeBSD 8 EA ownership is useful, but its EA content scan is a stub.
- Donor flag bits conflict; preserve/refuse before PS3 semantics are established.
- Key kind/layer must be explicit; exclude legacy UI secret logging.
- Orphan/duplicate preservation policy must precede destructive disposition.
- Generic repair journals must cover dirty originals and directory/EA payload.
- No hidden writes from destructors; every durable transition needs recovery.
- FAT/VFLASH, physical hosts and native console profiles qualify independently.
- Complete suite includes acquisition/recovery/backup/migration and FAT/VFLASH;
  a first UFS2 milestone cannot silently drop those tracks.
